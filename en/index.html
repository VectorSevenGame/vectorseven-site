How we work

Every product is built on three principles.

1

Fun first

Short sessions, instant feedback and a real sense of progress. People should come back because they want to, not because they have to.

2

Accurate content

In our learning products we prepare every fact with care and fix mistakes as soon as they are reported.

3

Respect for users

No forced accounts, no unnecessary data collection. Ads, if any, are balanced; purchases are transparent.
